# Privacy Policy — Down Under Bird Collector

_Last updated: [date]_

Down Under Bird Collector ("the app") is made by [your name]. This policy
explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## What the app stores

When you save a sighting, the app stores the photo, the species you chose,
the date and time, any notes you write and, if you allow it, the location
where you saw the bird.

**All of this stays on your device.** It is saved in the app's private
storage and included in your normal iPhone backups. We don't run servers
that receive it, and we can't see it.

## Permissions

- **Camera**: used only to take photos of birds when you tap the shutter.
- **Location (while using the app)**: used only to attach a location to a
  sighting you're saving. You can save sightings without a location.
- **Photos**: when you pick a photo from your library, the app reads only
  the photo you chose, including its date and location if it has them.

## Data from other services

Species names come from eBird and species photos from iNaturalist. These are
bundled with the app or loaded from iNaturalist's image servers when you
browse the field guide; loading an image tells those servers your IP
address, as any web image does. The app doesn't send them anything about
you or your sightings.

## Nearby birds (eBird)

If you add an eBird API key, the Nearby tab asks eBird (Cornell Lab of
Ornithology) for birds reported near you. That request includes your
location rounded to about 1 km and your API key — nothing about your
sightings. Without a key, nothing is sent.

## What we don't do

No accounts (unless cloud sync is enabled — see below), no analytics, no
advertising, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.

## Deleting your data

Delete individual sightings in the app, or everything at once in
*Collection → Settings → Delete all sightings*. Deleting the app also
removes all its data from the device.

## Cloud sync (only if offered)

If a version of the app offers sign-in, your sightings, photos and email
address are stored with Google Firebase so they sync between your devices.
You can delete your account and all synced data at any time in Settings.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal
information from anyone.
